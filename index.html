Privacy by Design: All audio and lighting calculation formulas execute strictly locally in volatile device memory (RAM). Zero calculation inputs or project specifications are transmitted to external servers.

1. Introduction

Amp Ohm is developed by AudioXpert to provide sound engineers, audio operators, DJs, and stage lighting technicians with fast, reliable calculation tools. These include loudspeaker impedance (Series, Parallel, and Series-Parallel), amplifier power and headroom matching, top speaker & crossover matching, and DMX 512 fixture addressing. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparent, privacy-centric data practices.

2. Information and Data Handling

To provide complete transparency, we distinguish how different categories of data are processed:

  • Calculator Inputs & Outputs: Any numerical parameters entered into calculators (wattage ratings, speaker impedances, driver counts, headroom multipliers, or DMX channels) are processed transiently in device RAM. They are not stored on remote servers or linked to your personal identity.
  • Locally Stored Preferences: Amp Ohm uses secure on-device sandboxed storage (SharedPreferences) solely for theme setting, language selection, haptic feedback toggle, local calculation history, 60-minute rewarded ad pass timestamp (ampohm_reward_pass_expiry_ms), and cached subscription entitlement status (rc_is_premium, rc_active_tier, rc_expiration_date).
  • Information Processed by Third-Party SDKs: Integrated SDKs (Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, RevenueCat, and Firebase) process standardized telemetry and anonymous device identifiers necessary to deliver ads, process in-app purchases, verify entitlements, and retrieve remote configuration flags.
  • Data Amp Ohm Itself Collects: Amp Ohm does not require account creation, registration, social login, passwords, phone numbers, or user profiles.

3. Calculator Data

All mathematical calculations and formulas run entirely on your local device. Your audio rig configurations, stage setups, speaker wiring combinations, and calculation results remain strictly on your phone. No project files or calculation histories are transmitted to AudioXpert or any third party.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Amp Ohm is supported by advertising delivered via Google AdMob (Google LLC):

  • Banner Ads: Non-intrusive banner ads displayed at the bottom of calculation result screens for free-tier users.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Opt-in video ads allowing free-tier users to watch a short ad to unlock a 60-minute ad-free pass across all calculators.
  • Ad-Free for Subscribers: Active Amp Ohm Pro subscribers receive a 100% ad-free experience.
  • Data Processing by Google AdMob: Google Mobile Ads SDK may process anonymous device identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP addresses for approximate location and fraud prevention, hardware specs, and ad interaction metrics. Review Google’s Privacy & Terms.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play & RevenueCat)

Amp Ohm offers an optional "Amp Ohm Pro" upgrade for 100% ad-free usage and unrestricted access:

  • Payment Processing by Google Play: All purchases are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing (Google LLC). AudioXpert never collects or stores your cards, bank details, or UPI credentials.
  • Verification via RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat SDK (RevenueCat, Inc.) to validate Google Play receipts and verify the active "ampohm Pro" entitlement.
  • Available Plans: Pro Monthly (ampohm_pro_monthly, fallback ₹29 / Month) and Pro Yearly (ampohm_pro_annual, fallback ₹199 / Year) auto-renewing subscriptions.
  • Renewal & Cancellation: Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled in Google Play Store (Account > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions) at least 24 hours before renewal. Pro access remains active until the end of the paid period, and active purchases can be restored on any device using "Restore Purchases".

6. Data Security

All external network communications (Google Play Billing, RevenueCat, Google AdMob, and Firebase) occur over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, and device preferences remain inside sandboxed OS storage.

7. Children’s Privacy

Amp Ohm is a professional utility for audio engineers, DJs, and lighting technicians. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) – Advertising delivery for free-tier users.
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC) – Subscription payment processing.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) – Subscription entitlement & receipt verification.
  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore (Google LLC) – Anonymous read-only remote configuration (app_config/ads_settings and app_config/version_control).

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

AudioXpert may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version is always available inside the app’s Settings & Information section and on this page.
